01THE WORK

The story
beneath the story.

A life cannot be reduced to a list of milestones.

The moments that make a book worth reading often appear after the expected answer: a decision, a doubt, a person, a detail nearly left out. I know how to make room for those moments and how to give them shape.

I work directly with you, from our first conversation to the final page.

02THE APPROACH

A PERSONAL PROCESS

Listen.
Shape. Write.

No handover between interviewer, strategist and writer. You work with me throughout.

I

Listen

I conduct the interviews myself. We follow the important questions, including the ones you may not have expected.

II

Shape

I find the throughline and build a structure that holds your experience, your ideas and the readers you want to reach.

III

Write

I write the manuscript, in your voice and with my craft. Your feedback guides the refinements until the book feels like yours.

04THE TRUST

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Some stories need
a quieter room.

Ghostwriting is intimate work. Confidentiality is part of the process, and we agree its terms before detailed work begins. I do not share your name, conversations or manuscript to promote my work without your permission.

I take on only a small number of books each year, giving each person and each story my full attention.
